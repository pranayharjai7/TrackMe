# TrackMe Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 28, 2026

Welcome to TrackMe! This policy explains how we handle your data to provide you with a seamless personal workout tracking experience. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your information.

## What Data We Collect

TrackMe collects the following types of information to power its features:

*   **Account Information:** Your email address, authentication details, and related sign-in provider metadata.
*   **Fitness & Workout Data:** Details about your workouts, including plans, exercises, sets, reps, weights, durations, distance, speed, incline, personal records, and historical progress.
*   **Health & Activity Data:** Metrics such as heart rate, steps, calories, distance, sleep/readiness scores, body measurements, and any data from Health Connect that you explicitly grant us permission to access or write.
*   **Device & Sync Data:** App-generated device identifiers, Wear OS connection status, FCM push notification tokens, and synchronization status.
*   **App Diagnostics:** Information to help us maintain app stability, such as crash reports, non-fatal errors, debugging logs, ANRs, and performance metrics.
*   **Analytics Data:** Aggregated app interaction and device information collected via Firebase Analytics (if enabled for your release).

## How We Use Your Data

Your data enables us to:

*   Deliver core features: workout planning, logging, progress analytics, and Wear OS data synchronization.
*   Securely manage your account and associate data with you.
*   Ensure your workout and health data is consistent across your devices.
*   Provide timely notifications for workouts, rest timers, sync status, and app updates.
*   Enhance app stability, diagnose issues, and resolve bugs efficiently.
*   Understand overall product usage to continuously improve your experience.

## Third-Party Services

TrackMe integrates with the following services, each with their own robust privacy and security policies:

*   **Supabase:** For secure authentication, database storage, and backend synchronization.
*   **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM):** For delivering push notifications.
*   **Firebase Crashlytics:** For essential crash and error reporting.
*   **Firebase Analytics:** For understanding app usage patterns (if enabled).
*   **Google Health Connect & Wear OS APIs:** For accessing and syncing your health and workout data, with your explicit permission.

## Data Storage and Security

Your workout and account data is primarily stored locally on your device. When you enable sync, this data is securely transmitted and stored in Supabase. We employ HTTPS for all network communications and utilize Supabase's Row Level Security (RLS) to ensure you can only access your own data.

**Your security is paramount.** Please do not share your device, account credentials, or exported data with untrusted individuals.

## Data Deletion

You have control over your data:

*   **Local Data:** You can delete local app data by signing out, uninstalling the app, or clearing your device's app storage.
*   **Cloud Data:** To request the deletion of your cloud account data, please contact us via the developer support email listed on the Google Play Store page.

*Note: Certain diagnostic records may be retained for a limited period after cloud data deletion for essential security, fraud prevention, debugging, legal compliance, or backup purposes.*

## Permissions

TrackMe requests only the Android permissions strictly necessary for its functionality:

*   **Notifications:** To send workout reminders and status updates.
*   **Network Access:** For syncing data and accessing backend services.
*   **Foreground Services:** For continuous workout tracking and timers.
*   **Sensors & Activity Recognition:** To accurately capture workout metrics.
*   **Wear OS Communication:** To sync data with your Wear OS devices.
*   **Health Connect:** To integrate with your health data (optional, user-controlled).

You can manage Health Connect permissions at any time through your Android device's settings.

## Children's Privacy

TrackMe is not designed for or intended for use by children under the age of 13.

## Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our app or services. The most current version will always be available at the privacy policy URL listed in the Google Play Console.

## Contact Us

For any privacy-related inquiries or data deletion requests, please reach out to the developer contact email provided on the TrackMe Google Play Store page.
